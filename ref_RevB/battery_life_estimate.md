## 0. What the Rev B netlist actually says
- Power tree: VBAT -> Q1 (AO3401A reverse-protect) -> Q3 FS8205A + U18 BQ29700 -> U1 BQ24075 (VSYS) -> U2 TPS63802 (MODE pin tied GND = power-save) -> `+3V3_REG` -> R_MEAS_3V3 0R -> `+3V3_SYS`.
- Always on `+3V3_SYS`: ESP32 module, **HLK-LD2410S (J_RADAR1 pin 1, no load switch)**, SHT40 (JP1), pull-ups. On VSYS: TMR3102 (drives TPS63802 EN via `PWR_EN`). On VBAT: MAX17048.
- Switched by TPS22919 (100k pull-downs, OFF in deep sleep): `+3V3_CAM` (U5 -> U6 2.8 V + U7 1.5 V LDOs -> **OV5640** on J6), `+3V3_SD` (U4), `FAN_VCC` (U10, then Q2 AO3400A PWM low-side).
- Fan and 1.5 V/2.8 V camera rails are LDO/linear: their current is drawn 1:1 from the 3.3 V rail.
- Charger: R3 2.2k on ISET -> ICHG = 890/2.2k = **405 mA**; R2 2.2k on ILIM -> USB input limit = 1610/2.2k = 732 mA.
- FB divider R6 56k / R7 10k: VOUT = 0.5 x (1+5.6) = 3.30 V, bleed 3.3/66k = **50 uA**.

Share of typical standby (battery side): radar 57%, FB divider 24%, ESP32 6%, TPS63802 IQ 5%, gauge+charger+prot+TMR 6%

Switched-rail leakage check: with EN low the three TPS22919 pass ~20 nA each [ASM], U6/U7 sit behind U5 (unpowered), the fan Q2 has a 100k gate pull-down and FAN_VCC is open. **Result: CAM/SD/FAN contribute ~0.06 uA total (conservative 3 uA).** Caveats: keep DVP/SDIO GPIOs high-Z while rails are off (back-powering through ESD diodes), and verify the 100k pull-downs hold EN low when the ESP32 pins float in deep sleep (GPIO_audit says yes).
Tamper loop: R44 1M to 3V3 with TAMPER_DRV held low = 3.27 uA static (3.3 V / 1.01 Mohm), about 3.8 uA at the battery. Pulsing TAMPER_DRV (e.g. 1 ms/s) cuts this to ~0.004 uA, but needs the LP core/timer to run the pulse, so the net saving is ~2-3 uA.
**Unverified risk:** BQ24075 TS pin biases TH1 (10k NTC) with a ~50 uA source; if that source stays on with VBUS absent it adds 50 uA, a bigger term than the whole MCU. Measure on the first Rev B board (R_MEAS_BAT1 / R_MEAS_3V3 0R links exist for this).
