## 0. What the Rev C netlist actually says
- Power tree: VBAT -> Q1 (AO3401A reverse-protect) -> Q3 FS8205A + U18 BQ29700 -> U1 BQ24075 (VSYS) -> U2 TPS63802 (MODE pin tied GND = power-save) -> `+3V3_REG` -> R_MEAS_3V3 0R -> `+3V3_SYS`.
- Always on `+3V3_SYS`: ESP32 module, **HLK-LD2410S (J_RADAR1 pin 1, no load switch)**, SHT40 (JP1), pull-ups. On VSYS: TMR3102 (`TMR_OUT`). On VBAT: MAX17048.
- Rev C: TPS63802 EN = `EN_TPS` = TMR_OUT | WAKE | HOLD through three BAT54WS Schottkys, R_EN_PD1 100k to GND. TMR_OUT also feeds R_TMR 470k/1M -> IO13. Keys: VSYS -> SW_L -> WAKE_MID -> SW_R -> WAKE (R_WAKE_PD1 1M), 47k/100k sense dividers -> IO0 / IO4.
- Switched by TPS22919 (100k pull-downs, OFF in deep sleep): `+3V3_CAM` (U5 -> U6 2.8 V + U7 1.5 V LDOs -> **OV5640** on J6), `+3V3_SD` (U4), `FAN_VCC` (U10, then Q2 AO3400A PWM low-side).
- Fan and 1.5 V/2.8 V camera rails are LDO/linear: their current is drawn 1:1 from the 3.3 V rail.
- Charger (Rev C, ILIM mode EN2 = HI / EN1 = LO): R3 887R on ISET -> ICHG = 890/887 = **1003 mA**; R2 1.13k on ILIM -> input limit = 1610/1.13k = **1425 mA**; R49 68k on TMR.
- FB divider R6 56k / R7 10k: VOUT = 0.5 x (1+5.6) = 3.30 V, bleed 3.3/66k = **50 uA**.

## 5a. Rev C standby change vs Rev B

- typical: Rev B **210.2 uA** -> Rev C **247.8 uA** at the battery (+37.6 uA, +17.9 %)
- conservative: Rev B **713.3 uA** -> Rev C **760.7 uA** at the battery (+47.4 uA, +6.6 %)
- Runtime effect (603450, 3 x 10 min Wi-Fi, fan 50 %): 4.67 d (Rev C) - standby is ~3 % of the daily budget, so the change is small; standby-only 603450: 149 d (Rev B) -> 128 d (Rev C).
- Chord-latched, magnet OFF (TMR_OUT low, HOLD high, pad-held in deep sleep): R_EN_PD1 is fed from +3V3_SYS through D_HOLD1 = 31 uA on the 3.3 V rail (31 uA at the battery) instead of the 37.5 uA VSYS terms above; the TMR divider is then unbiased. Standby total in this state: 241 uA.
- Both keys held (only while pressed; the board is ON): R_WAKE_PD1 1M = 3.7 uA plus the two 47k/100k sense dividers = 50 uA -> **+54 uA while pressed** (EN pull-down current is shared with TMR_OUT/HOLD). A 2 s chord costs 0.030 uAh.
- SW_L alone while the board is OFF: VSYS -> 47k -> SENSE_L -> 100k (25 uA) and IO0's clamp diode into the unpowered +3V3_SYS (<= ~60 uA) only while the key is held. SW_R alone draws nothing. Board OFF with no key pressed: Rev C adds 0 uA (all three diodes and both dividers are unbiased).
- Board OFF (magnet OFF, no HOLD): TMR3102 + MAX17048 + BQ24075 + BQ29700 = 13.5 uA typ, unchanged from Rev B.

Why these values: ICHG is held near 1/3 C of the ceiling cell (fast, but well under the 0.5 C = 1.5 A limit even at the KISET maximum, 0.37 C). R3 must be >= 590 ohm (DS range) and 1 % or better (DS note on the RISET short test): 887R 0.1 % thin film (C852938; the 1 % thick-film 887R has no JLC stock). R2 1.13k is the nearest E96 value that keeps R2 >= 1.1 kOhm (DS minimum) at -1 %; 1.1k could drop to 1.089 kOhm. R49 68k is inside the 18-72 kOhm DS range.

Input budget on USB (VSYS ~5.0 V): charge 1003 mA + clip load 255 mA (360 mA on 3.3 V) = **1259 mA**, under the minimum limit 1314 mA. Worst burst (573 mA on 3.3 V = 407 mA at VSYS) gives 1410 mA: above the limit, so DPPM trims the charge current for the burst (system first, battery supplement if needed) and the timer slows in proportion.

### Charge time and timer margin (from empty, 3000 mAh)

Model [ASM]: constant current until the CV point, then an exponential taper to ITERM = 0.1 x ICHG. CV entry at 80 % of capacity (healthy cell at ~C/3); a pessimistic case uses 70 % (aged or cold cell). The fast-charge timer runs at full speed in CC and CV and only slows during DPPM, VIN-DPM or thermal regulation [DS 9.3.5.6], so throttling cannot cause a false timer fault.

| Case | ICHG (mA) | CC (h) | CV taper (h) | Total (h) | Timer (h) | Margin |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| Typical (typ ICHG, typ KTMR) | 1003 | 2.39 | 1.53 | **3.92** | 9.07 | **2.31x** |
| Tolerance worst (min ICHG, min KTMR) | 899 | 2.67 | 1.71 | **4.38** | 6.80 | **1.55x** |
| Aged cell, typ parts | 1003 | 2.09 | 2.29 | **4.39** | 9.07 | **2.07x** |
| Aged cell + tolerance worst | 899 | 2.34 | 2.56 | **4.90** | 6.80 | **1.39x** |

Result: about **3.9 h** for a full charge of a 3000 mAh cell at 1.0 A (CC 2.4 h + CV 1.5 h). The 68k timer gives 9.1 h typical: 2.3x margin with typical parts and 1.55x with every tolerance at its worst; only the stacked aged-cell + worst-tolerance case drops to ~1.4x (then the charger flags a fault and stops; re-plugging USB restarts it). Rev B's 47k (6.3 h typ, 4.7 h min) would fault a 3000 mAh charge at 1.0 A in the worst case.
Pre-charge: a cell below 3.0 V needs roughly 1-2 % of capacity (60 mAh at 2 %) to reach VLOWV 3.0 V. Typical: 36 min at 99 mA against a 54 min timer; worst: 46 min at 79 mA against a 41 min timer, so a fully over-discharged 3000 mAh cell can trip the pre-charge timer (charger fault, re-plug restarts it). Pre-charge current is fixed at ~10 % of ICHG by R3, and R49 is near its 72k maximum, so this is a limit of the BQ24075 at this cell size. Measure on the real cell.

- U1 dissipation at VIN 5.0 V, VBAT 3.0 V, 1003 mA: 2.01 W -> +89 C (RthJA 44.5 C/W, JEDEC board; this board has thermal vias under U1). Above TJ 125 C the charger cuts current (thermal regulation) and the timer slows to match.
- U1 dissipation at VIN 5.0 V, VBAT 3.7 V, 1003 mA: 1.30 W -> +58 C (RthJA 44.5 C/W, JEDEC board; this board has thermal vias under U1). Above TJ 125 C the charger cuts current (thermal regulation) and the timer slows to match.
